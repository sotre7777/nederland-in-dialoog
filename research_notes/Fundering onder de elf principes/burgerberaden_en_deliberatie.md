# Burgerberaden en deliberatie: wat Nederlandse burgers concluderen onder gestructureerde omstandigheden

Scope: Nederland only. Evidence from burgerberaden, G1000's, burgerfora, Participatieve Waarde Evaluaties (PWE) and SCP studies, read as evidence for the eleven candidate shared principles (Inkomen, Vangnet, Pensioen, Wonen, Zorg, Onderwijs, Veiligheid, Immigratie, Leefomgeving/klimaat, Bevolking, Defensie). Throughout, "citizens recommended" is kept separate from "government adopted." Items marked (unverified) were seen only in search results or secondary summaries and not confirmed from the primary document.

Mapping of the evidence to the eleven principles (summary, details and sources in the sections below):
- Leefomgeving/klimaat: strongest evidence base by far (Nationaal Burgerberaad Klimaat 2025, PWE Klimaatraadpleging 2021, Haarlem/Utrecht/Zwolle/Gelderland burgerberaden, Commissie Brenninkmeijer 2021).
- Zorg: Burgerforum "Keuzes in de zorg" 2018 (Radboudumc/EUR), PWE corona-versoepelingen 2020 (30,000 participants).
- Pensioen: NIDI/Netspar vignette study on zware beroepen and AOW-leeftijd (2020), not a deliberative format but explicit trade-off design.
- Wonen, Leefomgeving (platteland, mobiliteit): G1000 Zuid-Holland 2023 (113 proposals).
- Immigratie: SCP 2025 (survey, not deliberative); no completed Dutch deliberative burgerberaad on migration found. Participants of the NBK and minister Heerma openly suggested asylum as the next topic.
- Inkomen, Vangnet, Onderwijs, Veiligheid, Bevolking, Defensie: no Dutch deliberative mini-public evidence found in this research (see Gaps).
- Cross-cutting: Burgerforum Kiesstelsel 2006 (democratic institutions), Bureau Burgerberaad adoption statistics (2025), SCP polarization studies (2022).

## Key question 1: Nationaal Burgerberaad Klimaat 2025 — who, how long, what it prioritized, what the cabinet adopted, did participants accept costs, what the evaluation said about opinion shifts

### Takeaway
175 lottery-selected citizens met over seven weekends (Jan–Sep 2025) and on 1 Dec 2025 delivered 13 main recommendations (82 sub-recommendations) focused on food, travel and consumer goods; on 29 May 2026 the cabinet said it would implement 41 of 82 (half), with about a quarter still "to be decided" and the rest not adopted, and critics noted many adopted items were already existing plans. Participants accepted cost-bearing measures such as a CO2/nitrogen levy on agriculture and a plastic levy, and the independent evaluation found the assembly did not bring opinions closer together but did increase mutual understanding.

### Cited Findings

Who and how long
- 175 participants selected by lottery to reflect the Dutch population on age, gender, residence and climate views; drawn from about 4,000 volunteers who answered letters sent to 70,000 people; they met for seven weekends with work visits, experts and people with lived experience; childcare and interpreters arranged; the assembly cost just over 6 million euros — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Recruitment detail from the independent evaluation (Report 1, Radboud/Tilburg/Haagse Hogeschool, published 22 April 2026): sample of 125,000 BAG addresses narrowed to 69,977 invitees; letters sent 14–15 Nov 2024 plus reminder; 5.82% (4,071 people) signed up, 3,663 available on all days; 175 drawn via the LEXIMIN algorithm on five criteria (gender, age, education, province, climate attitude); migration background could not be used for legal reasons; climate-attitude reference data from a Sept 2024 Ipsos I&O panel of 2,024 respondents — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 1](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-1-gebundeld)
- Representativeness: at the start the group was representative on the five lottery criteria; middle categories of climate opinion were well represented; political interest was much higher among participants (86.09% "tamelijk/zeer geïnteresseerd" vs 54.09% in the population); satisfaction with democracy slightly lower (5.69/10 vs 5.96/10) — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 1](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-1-gebundeld)
- Dropouts: 73 of the 175 originally drawn did not become participants (largest reason: 31 could not attend all days); replacements before the start matched on lottery criteria with climate attitude as top priority; no replacements after the start; three participants never attended — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 1](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-1-gebundeld)
- Duration: process ran from 18 Jan to 13 Sep 2025, six meetings plus one extra day, mostly two-day weekends; the report notes participation "requires a strong commitment of time and energy"; participants were paid through a volunteer allowance which the report calls unsustainable — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 1](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-1-gebundeld)
- Participants stay together for another year to monitor the government's response; the cabinet had to respond within half a year — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-ieno/burgerberaad-klimaat-levert-13-klimaatadviezen)

What the recommendations prioritized (citizens' side)
- Advice presented 1 Dec 2025: 13 recommendations supported by at least 75% of members, 10 more with at least 50% support; themes: reducing climate impact of food, travel and consumer goods; CE Delft estimated the package could cut emissions by 4% to 12% — [DutchNews, Dec 2025](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/12/climate-panel-calls-for-action-on-food-waste-and-train-tickets)
- Specific recommendations: a tax on farm-based CO2 and other emissions with some revenue recycled to agricultural sustainability (large majority support); supermarkets to cut prices on goods near sell-by date and donate surplus to food banks; restaurants encouraged to offer takeaway containers; a right to repair (broad support); scrap VAT on international rail tickets, easier cross-border rail booking, more support for working from home and cycling — [DutchNews, Dec 2025](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/12/climate-panel-calls-for-action-on-food-waste-and-train-tickets)
- Also among the 13: making a quarter of farmland organic; a longer product guarantee; stimulating train travel; taxing CO2 and nitrogen emissions from agriculture — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-ieno/burgerberaad-klimaat-levert-13-klimaatadviezen)
- Full advice document: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/documenten/rapporten/2025/12/01/advies-nationaal-burgerberaad-klimaat (linked from Binnenlands Bestuur; not fetched here, so the per-recommendation vote percentages are unverified)

What the cabinet adopted (government's side)
- On 29 May 2026 the cabinet said it would carry out 41 of the 82 recommendations; for about a quarter it is still checking feasibility and funding; many of the adopted items were already planned (e.g., right to repair, already agreed in Brussels; subsidy to trade in petrol/diesel cars for a second-hand EV, already presented in April 2026) — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Not adopted: the goal of a quarter of agriculture organic by 2030 (cabinet: unrealistic); black warning labels on unhealthy food (cabinet prefers a European route). Under review: a Spanish-style anti-food-waste law; financial support for farmers switching to organic (depends on funding). Minister Van Veldhoven read the advice as support for a plastic levy, not yet introduced — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Evaluation Report 2 (published 30 Sept 2026): recommendations were sorted into "Wel overnemen," "Wordt later besloten," "Niet overnemen"; 18 of the 82 are still to be decided; the cabinet says it wants to implement "in ieder geval de helft"; the Dutch response is about 50 pages (vs 161 Scotland, 157 Ireland), written at B1 level, one A4 per recommendation; the authors say it is too early to judge impact and recommend evaluating after two to three years — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 2](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-2-final)
- Cabinet will report on implementation over the next two years; the Tweede Kamer will debate the advice — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- The last time national government received citizens' advice was 2006 (Burgerforum Kiesstelsel) — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)

Did participants accept costs
- Yes on producer-side pricing: a CO2/nitrogen levy on agriculture had large majority support; supportive of a plastic levy per the minister's reading — [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/12/climate-panel-calls-for-action-on-food-waste-and-train-tickets); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Participant Marielle (Westland) said she had become more conscious of her consumption and now eats less meat — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- A critical outlet characterized the advice as "nog meer dwang en nog meer overheid" (unverified framing, opinion piece) — [Wynia's Week](https://www.wyniasweek.nl/nog-meer-dwang-en-nog-meer-overheid-het-nationaal-burgerberaad-klimaat-vertrouwt-de-burger-voor-geen-cent/?pdf=76155)

Evaluation: opinion shifts, process quality, legitimacy
- An April 2026 report by scientists found the assembly did not bring opinions closer together, but participants did gain more understanding of each other; researchers expect such assemblies could help with other sensitive issues such as nitrogen and asylum — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Participant survey (infographic, Nov 2025): group conversations respectful 93.4%; people listened well 89.6%; enough room to voice opinions 86.7%; facilitators neutral 80.0%; information rated 7.1–7.8 out of 10 on accessible/complete/diverse/useful; 81.5% felt adequately heard in choosing experts; "enough time" only 50.4% agreed (main concern); "recommendations feel like our own" 76.8%; voting method worked well 71.7%; willingness to accept the outcome (6+ on 0–10) 93.6%; overall rating 7.7; survey response rates 77.2%–94.9% — [Radboud Universiteit infographic](https://www.ru.nl/sites/default/files/2025-11/infographic-nbk-11_0.pdf)
- Participants were on average about as satisfied with democracy as the general population but somewhat more politically interested — [Radboud Universiteit infographic](https://www.ru.nl/sites/default/files/2025-11/infographic-nbk-11_0.pdf)
- General public awareness: about 25% had heard of the NBK (Dec 2025 and July 2026), only about 2% knew "(tamelijk) veel"; 8% knew the cabinet had responded; about 50% thought the NBK a (very) good idea, about 10% a (very) bad idea; among 161 respondents who knew of the response, 37% satisfied, 22% dissatisfied; of the dissatisfied, roughly 40% thought too few and roughly 40% too many recommendations would be implemented; satisfaction correlated with trust in government — [Radboud Universiteit, Rapport 2](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-2-final)
- Minister Heerma (BZK): compromise is the ideal, mutual understanding is already a good step; a participant suggested a citizens' assembly on asylum — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Research consortium: Kristof Jacobs (Radboud), Christine Bleijenberg (Haagse Hogeschool), Take Sipma and Frank Hendriks (Tilburg), funded by BZK, runtime from 30 June 2024 — [Radboud Universiteit project page](https://www.ru.nl/onderzoek/onderzoeksprojecten/onderzoek-en-evaluatie-nationaal-burgerberaad-klimaat-0)

### Inferences
- The NBK is the best Dutch example of a "zelfs als" statement: a representative group including skeptics endorsed pricing measures (agricultural emissions levy, plastic levy) and acceptance of the outcome was 93.6% even though opinions did not converge. The pattern is "accept the procedure and the package, keep your own opinion."
- The cabinet's "half adopted" headline overstates new policy: NOS and the evaluators note that many adopted items were already planned, so the net policy change attributable to the NBK is smaller and will only be measurable in 2–3 years.
- The main process complaint (only 50% felt they had enough time) is consistent with the G1000 Zuid-Holland finding below: time is the binding constraint, not information quality.

### Gaps
- The before/after participant surveys (chapter 3 and annex 5 of Report 1) with exact opinion-shift numbers were not retrieved; only NOS's summary ("no convergence, more understanding") is available. The final participant survey falls outside Report 2's period.
- Per-recommendation vote percentages for all 82 sub-recommendations and the exact count "not adopted" were not confirmed from the primary cabinet letter (Tweede Kamer document 2026D25917 / kst-1258644, listed in search results but not fetched).
- No evidence found on whether participants were asked to accept explicit consumer-side costs (higher meat or flight prices) and how they voted on those.

## Key question 2: Participatieve Waarde Evaluaties (TU Delft, Mouter et al.) — what trade-offs did citizens choose when forced to allocate budgets or accept constraints

### Takeaway
In the 2021 Klimaatraadpleging (10,000+ participants), about 75% of Dutch citizens form a middle group that supports ambitious climate policy if four conditions hold (industry targeted first, low incomes protected, polluter pays, benefits exceed costs); in the 2020 corona PWE (30,000 participants) citizens chose limited relaxations and rejected group-specific exemptions out of concern for unity. Across PWEs, 69–91% want expert and citizen input weighed together and about 80% want the method used more often.

### Cited Findings

Klimaatraadpleging 2021 (Leefomgeving/klimaat)
- More than 10,000 Dutch people took part in March 2021; report handed to Ed Nijpels on 17 June 2021 and presented to the Tweede Kamer; participants weighed 10 measures (offshore and onshore wind, kilometer charge, EV subsidy extension, livestock reduction, meat tax, higher gas energy tax, home insulation, tighter industry CO2 levy, more industry subsidies) — [TU Delft PVE case study](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- Measures highlighted as scoring well: offshore wind, tightening the industry CO2 levy, stimulating home insulation, more industry CO2-reduction subsidies, raising the air passenger tax, investing in green hydrogen — [TU Delft PVE case study](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- About 25% hold very strong views (either strongly opposed or "not ambitious enough"); about 75% form a middle group whose support depends on four conditions: (1) measures that directly affect private life (meat tax, onshore wind) only if other measures are clearly applied to the maximum and government strictly targets large polluting sectors; (2) low incomes protected and rich–poor gap not widened; (3) polluter pays; (4) benefits must outweigh costs and no better alternative — [TU Delft PVE case study](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation); [TU Delft press release June 2021](https://tudelft.nl/en/2021/tu-delft/broad-support-for-ambitious-climate-policy-if-four-conditions-are-met)
- Citizens feel industry and aviation escape climate policy through political connections; tougher action on them makes direct-impact measures easier to accept; kilometer charge feared as a burden on low-income car-dependent workers and for privacy, with higher fuel excise preferred by some — [TU Delft PVE case study](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- 91% want government to weigh both citizen and expert advice; 3% only citizens, 6% only experts; 80% want the method used more often; participation burden about 20–30 minutes — [TU Delft PVE case study](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- Gender split reported in the press: women saw more in a meat tax than men — [NOS, 2021](https://nos.nl/artikel/2372410-vrouwen-zien-meer-in-een-vleestaks-dan-mannen.html) (headline only; details unverified)

Corona-versoepelingen PWE, May 2020 (Zorg, Veiligheid in the sense of collective constraints)
- 30,000 people completed the PWE in the week before 6 May 2020 (organizers expected 10,000; 100,000 site visitors in seven days); participants took the cabinet's perspective and could not raise healthcare pressure by more than 50% — [TU Delft, May 2020](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2020/tu-delft/dutch-public-in-favour-of-limited-relaxation-of-coronavirus-measures)
- Top three chosen relaxations: contact professions (hairdressers) reopen; other businesses except hospitality/contact professions reopen; direct family from other households exempt from 1.5 m — [TU Delft, May 2020](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2020/tu-delft/dutch-public-in-favour-of-limited-relaxation-of-coronavirus-measures)
- Rejected: far-reaching relaxations that would overstretch healthcare; lifting restrictions for specific groups (the North, immune people, under-25s) because this would erode unity; reopening hospitality/entertainment over fears of drinking undermining distancing — [TU Delft, May 2020](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2020/tu-delft/dutch-public-in-favour-of-limited-relaxation-of-coronavirus-measures)
- 80% found the method an effective way to involve the public; 69% said scientists' advice should carry more weight than citizens', 26% equal, 5% citizens more — [TU Delft, May 2020](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2020/tu-delft/dutch-public-in-favour-of-limited-relaxation-of-coronavirus-measures)
- Peer-reviewed version: Mouter et al., PLOS ONE 2021 — [PMC8101923](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8101923) (not fetched; unverified details)

Other Dutch PWEs located but not fetched (unverified content)
- PWE Utrecht aardgasvrij / warmtetransitie report — [TU Delft file](https://filelist.tudelft.nl/TBM/PWE/Warmtetransitie%20Utrecht/PWE%20Utrecht%20aardgasvrij%20warmtetransitie%20rapport%20FINAL.pdf)
- PWE Vervoerregio Amsterdam (mobility budget allocation) — [TU Delft file](https://filelist.tudelft.nl/TBM/PWE/Een%20Participatieve%20Waarde%20Evaluatie%20voor%20de%20Vervoerregio%20Amsterdam%20definitief.pdf)
- A PWE-related annex submitted to the Tweede Kamer — [blg-1194384](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/blg-1194384.pdf)

### Inferences
- The four conditions from the Klimaatraadpleging are, in effect, citizen-authored "zelfs als" clauses: "we accept measures that hit our private lives, even if they cost us, provided industry goes first, the poor are shielded, and the polluter pays." They map directly onto Inkomen (protect low incomes), Leefomgeving and Vangnet.
- Both PWEs show a consistent fairness constraint: citizens reject exemptions for specific groups (corona) and reject burdens that fall unevenly (kilometer charge), even where those would be efficient. Equal treatment ranks above efficiency.
- PWE results are from self-selected online samples (weighted), so they are "informed opinion under constraint" rather than representative deliberation; the 25/75 split is a modeled segmentation.

### Gaps
- Full ranked results per measure (e.g., whether the meat tax or kilometer charge reached majority support) were not retrieved from the TU Delft case page.
- No budget-allocation PWE results were retrieved for the Utrecht or Amsterdam cases.

## Key question 3: G1000s and gemeentelijke/provinciale burgerberaden — recurring themes, syntheses, Commissie Brenninkmeijer

### Takeaway
Since 2022 roughly 40 to 71 Dutch burgerberaden have been held or planned (6,000+ participants); the G1000 Zuid-Holland 2023 case (housing, rural future, mobility; 113 proposals) shows the typical pattern: broadly representative on paper but with 2% invitation response, 34% no-show and 35% dropout, most adopted proposals overlapping with existing policy, and 14 months from handover to decision. The Brenninkmeijer commission (2021) recommended a representative citizens' forum deliberating intensively for half a year with a pre-agreed commitment on what politicians would do with the advice, which led to the NBK.

### Cited Findings

Scale of the Dutch burgerberaad movement
- Bureau Burgerberaad counted 40 citizen assemblies since 2022 with more than 6,000 participants over three years, with 13 more ongoing or scheduled for 2025 — [Binnenlands Bestuur, 26 June 2025](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/politiek-neemt-adviezen-burgerberaad-vaak-over)
- Ipsos I&O counted 71 burgerberaden in preparation, started or executed in the Netherlands (April 2025); BZK and VNG published the Handreiking Burgerberaden in autumn 2024, emphasizing representative selection and external evaluation — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O, 25 April 2025](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-i-en-o/een-goed-burgerberaad-kan-vertrouwen-in-bestuur-ten-goede)
- A De Haagse Hogeschool evaluation framework for local burgerberaden exists — [De Haagse Hogeschool](https://www.dehaagsehogeschool.nl/media/evaluatiekader-lokale-burgerberaden) (not fetched)

G1000 research (Leiden/Utrecht/VU, 2016)
- The BZK-supported study of 2014–2015 G1000s (Cohen et al., book "G1000. Ervaringen met burgertoppen," 31 March 2016) distinguishes a burgertop (citizens propose and commit to topics) from a burgerraad (aims to influence policy); lottery "is not a cure for democratic fatigue but can contribute"; meetings were productive for creativity and shared positions; Cohen concluded G1000s in their current form "did not necessarily narrow the gap between citizens and government" — [Universiteit Leiden, 2016](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/nieuws/2016/05/job-cohen-presenteert-eindrapport-g1000-onderzoek)

G1000 Zuid-Holland 2023 (Wonen, Leefomgeving, mobiliteit)
- Three assemblies (Jan–Mar 2023) on Wonen, Toekomst van het platteland, and Leefbaarheid/verkeer/vervoer; 460 selected by lot, 305 attended the opening, 199 the final assembly; only 2% of 75,000 invitations responded; no-show 34%, dropout 35% (higher than Drenthe and Gelderland); theoretically educated overrepresented (42% vs 31%) — [Provincie Zuid-Holland evaluatieonderzoek, Oct 2024](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf)
- Burgerakkoord of 113 proposals handed over 25 March 2023 (40 housing, 44 rural, 29 mobility); on 15 May 2024 Provinciale Staten adopted 41 in full, 52 in part, rejected 20 (18 of 20 for lack of provincial authority); most adopted proposals overlapped with existing or planned policy or the 2023 coalition agreement, "so the assembly produced little new or changed policy" — [Provincie Zuid-Holland evaluatieonderzoek](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf)
- Only about 45% of participants trusted their proposals would be taken into account (36% neutral, 17% doubtful; 30% survey response); preparation of three months judged too short (OECD suggests at least six); follow-up took about 14 months; participants were surprised by how long decision-making took — [Provincie Zuid-Holland evaluatieonderzoek](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf)

Haarlem burgerberaad CO2 (Leefomgeving)
- 109 Haarlemmers delivered 25 recommendations; the college worked 23 into concrete policy proposals; Ipsos found participants who experienced the procedure as fair accepted outcomes more often (procedural justice) and gained more trust in municipal climate policy (pre n=84, post n=54; figures not in text); the article is an Ipsos partner contribution, not independently tested — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O, 25 April 2025](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-i-en-o/een-goed-burgerberaad-kan-vertrouwen-in-bestuur-ten-goede)

Commissie Brenninkmeijer, "Betrokken bij klimaat" (March 2021)
- Recommended a representative citizens' forum discussing climate choices "intensively for half a year" (topics such as RES wind/solar plans and natural-gas-free homes); key condition: clarity in advance about what politicians will do with the advice, otherwise it "disappears in a drawer" and widens the gap; options: councils may deviate only with good arguments, or adopt in full if produced within agreed boundaries; traditional participation (information evenings) seen as unsatisfying — [NOS, 2021](https://nos.nl/l/2373547)
- Cabinet request to the commission, 29 Jan 2021 — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2021/01/29/kabinet-vraagt-adviescommissie-participatie-in-het-klimaatbeleid-te-onderzoeken); Kamerstuk — [kst-32813-1084](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-32813-1084.pdf) (not fetched)

Other local cases located but not fetched (unverified)
- Zwolle first burgerberaad lessons — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-ieno/de-lessen-uit-het-eerste-zwolse-burgerberaad)
- Heemstede burgerberaad evaluation (Feb 2025) and a TNO report on burgerberaden (2023) — [Heemstede evaluatie](https://gemeentebestuur.heemstede.nl/Vergaderingen/College-van-Burgemeester-en-Wethouders/2025/11-februari/09:30/Evaluatie-burgerberaad/Evaluatie-burgerberaad-1.pdf); [TNO rapport](https://gemeentebestuur.heemstede.nl/Vergaderingen/College-van-Burgemeester-en-Wethouders/2023/07-maart/09:30/Een-Heemsteeds-burgerberaad/TNO-rapport-burgerberaden-1.pdf)
- Gelderland: a provincial "vleesmotie" following a burgerberaad drew criticism from civil servants — [Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/carriere/omgevingswet/gelderse-ambtenaren-bekritiseren-vleesmotie) (headline only)

### Inferences
- Recurring recommendation themes across the Dutch cases are modest and practical (food waste, repair, rail, insulation, housing supply) rather than radical; citizens tend to propose things that are already within the policy mainstream, which explains both high "adoption rates" and evaluators' remark that little new policy results.
- The Zuid-Holland dropout/no-show figures (one in three each) and the NBK's 73 replacements of 175 show that representativeness is achieved on paper at selection but erodes in attendance; the universally observed bias is toward the higher-educated and politically interested.
- Brenninkmeijer's "commit in advance" condition is the single most repeated lesson (also in Bureau Burgerberaad's finding that 62% of assemblies had such a commitment).

### Gaps
- No synthesis document from G1000.nu or Bureau Burgerberaad listing recurring themes across all 40+ Dutch burgerberaden was retrieved; only the adoption statistics.
- Amersfoort, Uden, Steenwijkerland G1000 outcomes were not individually retrieved (only a Steenwijkerland council Q&A appeared in search results, unverified).
- No Dutch burgerberaad on Onderwijs, Veiligheid, Defensie, Inkomen, Vangnet or Bevolking was found.

## Key question 4: Deliberative evidence on pensions, health care priorities, housing and migration

### Takeaway
Dutch evidence outside climate is thin and mostly non-deliberative: the 2018 Burgerforum "Keuzes in de zorg" (24 citizens, three weekends) produced 16 criteria and accepted co-payments, lifestyle conditions and excluding cheap or non-necessary care while insisting on solidarity with the seriously ill; a 2020 NIDI/Netspar vignette study (2,714 respondents) found broad support for earlier AOW for heavy occupations (about 1–1.6 years) with the financing choice shaping the acceptable overall AOW age; on migration the SCP (2025) finds a broad nuanced middle but no completed Dutch burgerberaad exists.

### Cited Findings

Zorg
- Burgerforum "Keuzes in de zorg" (Radboudumc, Erasmus Universiteit, Radboud Universiteit; initiator Rob Baltussen): 24 citizens from all walks of life met over three weekends; manifest "Draagvlak voor lastige keuzes" presented 21 June 2018 in The Hague; 16 criteria for care choices; participants wanted citizen involvement in major themes such as lifestyle and personal responsibility, not every decision — [Erasmus School of Health Policy & Management, 22 June 2018](https://www.eur.nl/eshpm/nieuws/keuzes-maken-zorg-lastig-de-burger-wijst-politiek-de-weg)
- Trade-offs accepted: costs must be weighed against benefits (fear that otherwise "everything could be reimbursed"); address lifestyle first before gastric banding; cheap medication such as vitamins to be paid by patients; critical of reimbursing non-medically necessary treatments (cosmetic dental); co-payments for first treatments (e.g., physiotherapy) to prevent inappropriate use; strong solidarity with patients with serious illnesses — [ESHPM, 2018](https://www.eur.nl/eshpm/nieuws/keuzes-maken-zorg-lastig-de-burger-wijst-politiek-de-weg)
- Zorginstituut chair Arnold Moerkamp said the institute would look for new ways to involve citizens; Patiëntenfederatie supported active citizen involvement — [ESHPM, 2018](https://www.eur.nl/eshpm/nieuws/keuzes-maken-zorg-lastig-de-burger-wijst-politiek-de-weg)
- Manifest and participant brochure: [Radboudumc manifest](https://www.radboudumc.nl/getmedia/3e2f4dd8-beaf-4768-adb8-facc950f9629/Radboudumc_Burgerforum_Manifest_DEF.aspx); [Informatiebrochure](https://radboudumc.nl/getmedia/b638cbd7-7a52-4b17-bac3-29784185e489/Informatiebrochure-deelnemers.aspx) (not fetched)
- Corona PWE 2020 (see question 2) is the largest Dutch health-related trade-off exercise: 30,000 participants chose limited relaxations under a 50% healthcare-pressure cap — [TU Delft, May 2020](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/2020/tu-delft/dutch-public-in-favour-of-limited-relaxation-of-coronavirus-measures)

Pensioen
- Van Dalen, Henkens and Oude Mulders (ESB 9 July 2020, pp. 340–342; funded by Instituut Gak): factorial vignette survey in the LISS panel, 2,714 respondents, 80% response, representative; respondents judged for fictional 60-year-olds how many years before or after the AOW age a full benefit is reasonable — [ESB, 2020](https://esb.nu/brede-steun-voor-zware-beroepenregeling/al0qct58rhsbp8wxo2ikgot3edy)
- Results: heavy occupation −0.77 years; combined with starting work at 16 about 1.12 years earlier; very heavy occupations (stratenmaker, stukadoor, brandweer) 1.2–1.6 years earlier; chronic joint complaints −1.05, cardiovascular −1.03, psychological −0.84, diabetes −0.58, daily informal care −0.89; light occupation +0.33; part-time +0.20 — [ESB, 2020](https://esb.nu/brede-steun-voor-zware-beroepenregeling/al0qct58rhsbp8wxo2ikgot3edy)
- Financing trade-off: respondents favoring collective (non-actuarially-neutral) financing support a lower overall AOW age (−0.34); those favoring actuarially neutral financing support a higher AOW age (+0.41); shares choosing each option not reported — [ESB, 2020](https://esb.nu/brede-steun-voor-zware-beroepenregeling/al0qct58rhsbp8wxo2ikgot3edy)
- Related Netspar material located but not fetched: Netspar Brief 12 (2017), Brief 16 (2019), Design paper 150 (2020) — [Netspar Brief 16](https://www.netspar.nl/wp-content/uploads/D20190418_Netspar-Brief-16-WEB.pdf); [Design paper 150](https://www.netspar.nl//assets/uploads/D20200703_1P_Netspar_Design_paper-150_NL.pdf)

Wonen
- G1000 Zuid-Holland 2023: 40 housing proposals of 113 (see question 3); nearly three quarters of Dutch respondents think refugee arrivals make it harder for Dutch people to find housing (SCP 2025) — [Provincie Zuid-Holland](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf); [Hart van Nederland on SCP, 1 April 2025](https://www.hartvannederland.nl/politiek/debat/artikelen/nederlanders-genuanceerder-over-migratie-dan-gedacht)

Immigratie
- SCP (reported 1 April 2025; survey-based, not deliberative): 60% say the Netherlands has a moral obligation to take in refugees from war zones; about one third want borders closed to asylum seekers even if this conflicts with international agreements; 9% want more asylum seekers; a broad middle group recognizes both benefits and drawbacks, worries mainly about numbers but wants room for war refugees and labor migrants in shortage sectors; attitudes toward asylum have become more positive over twenty years; the SCP calls the debate "verhit" with opponents and proponents listening less to each other — [Hart van Nederland on SCP](https://www.hartvannederland.nl/politiek/debat/artikelen/nederlanders-genuanceerder-over-migratie-dan-gedacht)
- SCP Sociale en Culturele Ontwikkelingen 2024, chapter 7 on asylum and migration — [SCP](https://scp.nl/binaries/scp/documenten/publicaties/2024/03/27/sociale-en-culturele-ontwikkelingen-2024/SCO+2024+hoofdstuk+7+-+Asiel+en+migratie.pdf) (not fetched)
- Dutch participants in the EU Conference on the Future of Europe citizens' panel (2022) favored "humane migration" and a more independent Europe — [Parlement.com, Feb 2022](https://www.parlement.com/nieuws/202202/burgerpanel-conferentie-over-de-toekomst-menselijke-migratie-en-onafhankelijker) (headline only, unverified)
- NBK participants and researchers explicitly named asylum/migration as a next topic for a burgerberaad; NU headline: "Hoeven het niet eens te zijn" — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2616324); [Radboud project page](https://www.ru.nl/onderzoek/onderzoeksprojecten/onderzoek-en-evaluatie-nationaal-burgerberaad-klimaat-0)

### Inferences
- The 2018 Zorg burgerforum is the clearest non-climate "zelfs als" evidence: citizens accepted personal co-payments and lifestyle conditions (costs to themselves) in exchange for guaranteed solidarity with the seriously ill. That is a principle with an explicit price tag.
- On pensions, the vignette evidence shows citizens differentiate by burden (heavy work, health, care duties) rather than by a single age, and that willingness to accept a higher general AOW age depends on who pays for the exceptions.
- On migration, the SCP data describe a nuanced majority but no Dutch deliberative outcome exists; this is the biggest evidence gap among the eleven principles with high political salience.

### Gaps
- No Zorginstituut "Burgerforum Zorg" or "eerlijke verdeling van zorg" deliberative study was found beyond the 2018 Radboudumc forum; Zorginstituut's follow-up actions are undocumented here.
- No SER/Netspar deliberative mini-public on AOW-leeftijd was found; the available evidence is a vignette survey.
- No "Kiezen voor migratie" SCP citizen dialogue or Adviesraad Migratie "Migratiedialoog" document was located in this search; may exist under other titles (unverified).

## Key question 5: Does deliberation move Dutch citizens toward the middle (perception gap closing)? More in Common NL, Nederland in Gesprek, Nationale Dialoog, Het Grote Gesprek

### Takeaway
Dutch evidence shows a perception gap rather than a convergence effect: about three quarters of Dutch people think polarization is rising while the SCP finds no clear increase in hardened opinion differences (2022), and the NBK evaluation found no convergence of opinions after seven weekends of deliberation, only more mutual understanding. No Dutch More in Common, Nederland in Gesprek, Nationale Dialoog or Het Grote Gesprek outcome data were found in this research.

### Cited Findings
- SCP citizen-perspectives research (reported 29 Dec 2022; two surveys of 1,900 and 1,300+ respondents): about three quarters think polarization is rising, but researchers see no clear increase in hardened differences of opinion; there is more agreement on many topics than people assume; two thirds believe others can be trusted; opinions are perceived as more extreme mainly in media, social media and political debate; people experience less polarization in personal life except around corona vaccination — [NL Times, 29 Dec 2022](https://nltimes.nl/2022/12/29/dutch-people-think-polarization-increasing-research-shows-otherwise)
- Respondents felt small loud groups get disproportionate attention; most irritation directed at contrarian groups (conspiracy supporters, anti-vaccination, radical right, anti-democratic or racist groups), with some irritation at climate, LGBTQ and anti-racism advocates — [NL Times, 29 Dec 2022](https://nltimes.nl/2022/12/29/dutch-people-think-polarization-increasing-research-shows-otherwise)
- NBK evaluation (April 2026): the assembly did not bring opinions closer together, but participants gained more understanding of each other — [NOS, 29 May 2026](https://nos.nl/l/2616324)
- Klimaatraadpleging 2021: the 25% / 75% split (strong views vs conditional middle) is a segmentation of informed respondents, not a before/after measurement — [TU Delft](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- Haarlem burgerberaad: participants gained more trust in municipal climate policy pre vs post, per Ipsos (figures not published in text) — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-i-en-o/een-goed-burgerberaad-kan-vertrouwen-in-bestuur-ten-goede)
- G1000 2014–2015 research: G1000s "did not necessarily narrow the gap between citizens and government" — [Universiteit Leiden, 2016](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/nieuws/2016/05/job-cohen-presenteert-eindrapport-g1000-onderzoek)
- RUG blog series on polarization in the Netherlands (not fetched) — [RUG](https://www.rug.nl/feb/research/frn/blog/polarization-in-the-netherlands-part-two)

### Inferences
- The Dutch pattern is "understanding without convergence": deliberation changes how people see each other and the procedure (93.6% accept the NBK outcome), not primarily what they think. For a framework built on shared principles this suggests aiming at agreed procedures and conditions (the "four conditions" type) rather than agreed positions.
- The SCP perception-gap finding supports the framework premise that a nuanced majority exists on most topics, but the evidence that structured dialogue closes the gap is indirect.

### Gaps
- No Dutch More in Common report, "Nederland in Gesprek" results, Stichting Nationale Dialoog evaluation or "Het Grote Gesprek" outcome data were found; searches returned only theses and the SCP studies. These may exist under other names or were not indexed; treat as unverified.
- No Dutch deliberative-poll style before/after measurement with published effect sizes was found other than the (not retrieved) NBK chapter 3.

## Key question 6: Adoption rates of burgerberaad recommendations and what citizens said about legitimacy afterward

### Takeaway
Bureau Burgerberaad (2025) finds that in more than half of 40 Dutch assemblies at least 75% of advice was adopted and 12 assemblies had all advice adopted, but evaluators consistently warn that adoption often means relabeling existing policy; the 2006 Burgerforum Kiesstelsel (140 members, 8 months) was rejected outright by the cabinet in 2008, and in Zuid-Holland only 45% of participants trusted their proposals would count.

### Cited Findings
- Bureau Burgerberaad (reported 26 June 2025): across 40 assemblies since 2022, more than half had at least 75% of advice adopted; 12 had all advice adopted; in 62% of assemblies politicians committed in advance to treat outcomes as weighty advice (comparable to a Rekenkamer report); "als er geen of weinig politieke opvolging plaatsvindt, blijft de impact van een burgerberaad beperkt"; Eva Rovers: "De tijd, betrokkenheid en adviezen van inwoners verdienen meer dan een dankwoord"; poor follow-up can damage trust — [Binnenlands Bestuur, 26 June 2025](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/politiek-neemt-adviezen-burgerberaad-vaak-over)
- NBK: 41 of 82 adopted (29 May 2026), roughly a quarter pending, many adopted items pre-existing plans — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2616324); [Radboud Rapport 2](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-2-final)
- G1000 Zuid-Holland: 41 of 113 adopted in full, 52 in part, 20 rejected (18 for lack of competence); most adopted items overlapped with existing policy; 45% of participants trusted follow-up — [Provincie Zuid-Holland](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf)
- Haarlem: 23 of 25 recommendations worked into policy proposals — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-i-en-o/een-goed-burgerberaad-kan-vertrouwen-in-bestuur-ten-goede)
- Burgerforum Kiesstelsel 2006: commissioned by minister Pechtold; 140 members chaired by Jacobine Geel; random sample of 100,000 voters from the GBA, about 50,000 invited on 4 Feb 2006, nearly 4,000 registered, about 3,000 attended nine information meetings, over 1,700 applied, 140 drawn by lot on 14 March 2006 with equal gender split and provincial proportionality; installed 24 March 2006, advice in November 2006; three phases (education, consultation of other citizens, drafting); recommended proportional representation with one vote for either a party or a candidate, abolition of the 25% preference threshold, and largest-remainder seat allocation; in April 2008 the Balkenende IV cabinet "saw no reason to adopt the forum's advice" — [Parlement.com](https://www.parlement.com/id/vhnnmt7ltkw7); [Parlement.com news April 2008](https://parlement.com/nieuws/200804/kabinet-voelt-niet-voor-wijziging-kiesstelsel)
- A later cabinet (2020) proposed a "list or person" vote reminiscent of the 2006 advice — [NOS, 2020](https://nos.nl/artikel/2339112-kabinet-voortaan-kiezen-voor-lijst-of-persoon) (headline only; the link to the 2006 advice is an inference, unverified)
- Legitimacy among participants: NBK willingness to accept outcome 93.6%, recommendations "feel like our own" 76.8% — [Radboud infographic](https://www.ru.nl/sites/default/files/2025-11/infographic-nbk-11_0.pdf)
- Legitimacy among the public: about 50% of Dutch think the NBK a (very) good idea, 10% bad; satisfaction with the cabinet response is split and correlates with trust in government — [Radboud Rapport 2](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-2-final)
- Opinion pieces warn "beter geen burgerberaad dan een slecht burgerberaad" — [Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/integriteit/beter-geen-burgerberaad-dan-een-slecht-burgerberaad) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Headline adoption rates (75–100%) are high because recommendations tend to be moderate and because "adoption" includes already-planned policy; the Zuid-Holland and NBK evaluators both make this point. The 2006 Kiesstelsel case shows that when a recommendation requires real institutional change, outright rejection is possible, which is exactly the "verdwijnt in een la" risk Brenninkmeijer warned about.
- The legitimacy effect is strongest among participants (93.6% accept) and weak in the general public (25% awareness, 8% aware of the cabinet response), so burgerberaden do not by themselves create society-wide "shared principles"; they create a documented, defensible package.

### Gaps
- No systematic count of recommendations actually implemented (as opposed to "adopted" or "overgenomen") was found for any Dutch case; evaluators say this needs 2–5 years.
- The Bureau Burgerberaad underlying dataset/report (bureauburgerberaad.nl) was not fetched; figures come from Binnenlands Bestuur's report of it.

## Methodological lessons on reading time, duration and representativeness (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
Dutch cases converge on: invitation response of 2–6%, lottery on 4–5 criteria, systematic overrepresentation of the higher-educated and politically interested, one in three no-shows or dropouts in provincial G1000s, 6–9 months of weekend sessions for national or provincial topics, at least six months of preparation, 20–30 minutes for an online PWE, and "too little time" as the participants' main complaint.

### Cited Findings
- NBK: 5.82% sign-up from about 70,000 invitations; 73 of 175 replaced before start; 50.4% felt they had enough time; six meetings plus one extra day over eight months — [Radboud Rapport 1](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-1-gebundeld); [Radboud infographic](https://www.ru.nl/sites/default/files/2025-11/infographic-nbk-11_0.pdf)
- Burgerforum Kiesstelsel: about 4,000 registrations from about 50,000 invitations (roughly 8%, computed), 140 members, March–November 2006 — [Parlement.com](https://www.parlement.com/id/vhnnmt7ltkw7)
- G1000 Zuid-Holland: 2% response to 75,000 invitations; 34% no-show, 35% dropout; theoretically educated 42% vs 31%; three months preparation too short; 14 months to decision — [Provincie Zuid-Holland](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/evaluatierapport-g1000zuid-holland-2023/evaluatieonderzoek-g1000zuid-holland-2023.pdf)
- Brenninkmeijer: intensive deliberation for half a year — [NOS, 2021](https://nos.nl/l/2373547)
- PWE: 20–30 minutes participation burden; attracts fewer participants than a poll but captures trade-offs — [TU Delft](https://tudelft.nl/en/tpm/pve/case-studies/climate-consultation)
- Ipsos lottery practice: stepwise weighted lottery with oversampling of groups less likely to participate, such as young people — [Binnenlands Bestuur / Ipsos I&O](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/bestuur-en-organisatie/ipsos-i-en-o/een-goed-burgerberaad-kan-vertrouwen-in-bestuur-ten-goede)
- Zorg burgerforum: 24 citizens, three weekends — [ESHPM, 2018](https://www.eur.nl/eshpm/nieuws/keuzes-maken-zorg-lastig-de-burger-wijst-politiek-de-weg)
- NBK cabinet response written at B1 reading level, one A4 per recommendation, about 50 pages — [Radboud Rapport 2](https://www.ru.nl/ru-bestanden/rapport-2-final)

### Inferences
- For a framework aimed at schools and gemeenten, the PWE format (20–30 minutes, forced trade-offs under a budget or capacity cap) is the most scalable proven Dutch instrument; the burgerberaad format is the most legitimate but requires 6+ months and roughly 6 million euros at national scale.
- Any Dutch deliberative exercise should expect and correct for a 2–6% response rate and an education/political-interest skew; climate attitude (or the equivalent attitude on the topic) as a lottery criterion was the NBK's key innovation for credibility with skeptics.

### Gaps
- No Dutch data were found on the volume of reading material given to participants (pages, hours) in any burgerberaad.
- No cost figures found for local burgerberaden; only the NBK's just over 6 million euros.
